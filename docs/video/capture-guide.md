# Capture guide — candidate demo (.co.uk)

Companion to `candidate-demo-script.md`. Read this before recording anything.

Walked the live signed-in product on 30 Jul 2026 to confirm each shot in the list is
actually achievable. **It is not, yet.** One blocker has to be cleared first, and it is
worth clearing rather than working around.

---

## Blocker: the account has no usable data

The script assumes a candidate with a history. The live account currently has:

| | State | Needed for |
|---|---|---|
| Interview sessions | **1**, scored **1/10** | Shots 5, 8 |
| Category averages | **0/10** across content, clarity, relevance, structure | Shot 5 |
| Latest signal | **Weak** | Shot 8 |
| Score trajectory | **a single point** | Shot 8 |
| Assessment centre sessions | 4 | Shots 6, 7 |

The one interview session is the deliberately non-serious test answer from earlier
QA. Recording shot 5 today would put **1/10 and a "Weak" readiness signal** on screen
in a video whose entire argument is that the product makes you better. Shot 8 is
simply impossible: a trend line needs more than one point.

### What to do about it

**Seed the account with real sessions before recording.** Specifically:

- **4 to 5 completed interview practice sessions**, same target role throughout, spread
  across a few days so the dates on the trend read naturally.
- Answers should be **genuinely decent but not perfect** — aiming for scores in the
  6 to 8 band. The recalibrated scorer will award these honestly.
- **Include one weaker session in the middle.** A trend that dips and recovers is far
  more credible than a straight climb, and it is what real improvement looks like.
- **One completed assessment centre** run end to end, so shots 6 and 7 have a real
  case study and a real report.

This costs your own OpenAI spend and roughly an hour. It is not optional: it is the
difference between a demo that argues its case and one that undermines it.

Use a plausible role such as **Graduate Analyst** rather than the current saved
profile, which is a specific senior fund-control job description and reads as
personal data.

## Per-shot capture notes

Recording settings: **1920×1080, 60fps**, browser chrome hidden, no bookmarks bar,
no notifications. Move the cursor deliberately and slightly slower than feels natural.

**Shot 3 — practice setup** (`/practice`)
Confirmed present. Hero reads "Interview preparation that makes a measurable
difference", with a live session preview panel showing what the coach assesses
(Answer substance, Delivery). Good frame: the role field mid-type.

**Shot 4 — live session** (`/practice/session`)
Confirmed. Voice + camera mode shows the question card, a "Recording..." state and a
live camera preview. Have a real question on screen, not a placeholder.

**Shot 5 — feedback panel**
Blocked until seeding is done. Once seeded, capture the overall score, the per-dimension
bars and the filler-word count. **This is the most important frame in the video** — it
is the proof beat the whole script builds to.

**Shot 6 — case study** (`/assessment-centre` → stage 1)
Confirmed. Stage 1 shows the brief with a **12:00** timer. Start recording before the
timer begins so the countdown is live on screen.

**Shot 7 — assessment centre report**
Needs one completed run. Capture overall score, readiness level and stage breakdown.

**Shot 8 — progress** (`/progress`)
Blocked until seeding. The page has everything the shot needs once data exists: score
trajectory chart, latest/average/best, category averages and session history.

**Shot 10 — closing loop**
Any clean practice question card, then hold on the logo. No data dependency.

## Things to avoid on camera

- The current saved profile contains a **real senior job description**. Change it before
  recording.
- Do not show the account email or avatar initial in the top right if avoidable.
- Nothing scored 1/10 or marked "Weak" anywhere in frame.
- No real company names in any target-role field.

1. **Seed the account** — 4 to 5 practice sessions plus one full assessment centre.

**Do not upgrade the Higgsfield plan until step 2 is done.** The billed month starts on

# Advertising concepts — English (.co.uk)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds. Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
**Voice:** British, same voice as the demos.
**Landing page:** see "Where these should land" below. This matters more than the creative.

Three concepts. Concept A is the one I would run first, and the reasoning is in the
section after it.

---

## Why these are built on Civil Service

Your own Search Console data, not a hunch. Over the two weeks to 25 July:

- **1,063 of 1,590 total site impressions** came from one page: `civil-service-interview-tips`
- Seven of the top ten queries were Civil Service terms: *civil service behaviours*,
  *civil service framework*, *civil service strengths*, *civil service competency scores*
- Average position 25, so almost no clicks — the demand is real and currently unserved
  **by you**, not absent

That is measured intent from people who already want this, in a niche with a hard annual
deadline. Advertising into demand you can see beats advertising into demand you hope
exists. It also means the ad and the seven-page cluster reinforce each other rather than
competing.

**Timing:** Civil Service and Fast Stream applications concentrate in autumn. An ad in
September or October meets people mid-application. The same ad in June meets nobody.

## Concept A — "The scores nobody explains"

**The insight:** candidates are marked 0–7 against published Behaviour descriptors and
almost none of them know it. They find out they scored a 3 after they have been rejected.
That gap is uncomfortable, specific, and true.

| Time | Shot | On screen text | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | **AI** — hands closing a laptop after reading a rejection email, no face | "You scored a 3." | "You scored a three." |
| 1.5–4.0 | **AI** — same person, still, looking at nothing | "Out of 7." | "Out of seven. And nobody tells you what a three was missing." |
| 4.0–7.5 | **CAPTURE** — practice session, question card visible | — | "So practise it first. Out loud." |
| 7.5–11.5 | **CAPTURE** — feedback panel, dimension scores landing | "Where you rambled." | "It tells you where you rambled. Whether your answer had a result in it." |
| 11.5–15.0 | **CAPTURE** — hold on logo + URL | "aicareermentor.co.uk" | "Before it's the real one." |

**Hook strength:** "You scored a 3" in the first 1.5 seconds is specific, unexplained
and slightly alarming. It reads as information rather than advertising, which is what
survives a scroll.

**Why this one first:** it is the only concept whose hook is a fact the audience
recognises immediately. If you can only afford to test one, test this.

## Concept B — "Said it out loud"

**The insight:** the gap between preparing and rehearsing. Broader appeal than Concept A,
so it works beyond Civil Service, but the hook is softer.

| Time | Shot | On screen text | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | **AI** — notes, highlighter, closed laptop at night | "40 hours of prep." | "You've done forty hours of prep." |
| 2.0–4.5 | **AI** — person opening mouth to speak to an empty room, stopping | "0 minutes out loud." | "And nought minutes actually saying it." |
| 4.5–8.5 | **CAPTURE** — voice + camera session recording | — | "This is somewhere to do that. On camera, like the real thing." |
| 8.5–12.5 | **CAPTURE** — feedback: filler count, pace | "You said 'um' 14 times." | "Then it tells you what you can't hear yourself doing." |
| 12.5–15.0 | **CAPTURE** — logo + URL | "aicareermentor.co.uk" | "Practise properly, before it counts." |

**Hook strength:** the "40 hours / 0 minutes" contrast is visual and instant. Weaker
than A because it describes a habit rather than a consequence.

**Note on the filler count:** use a real number from a real session. Do not invent "14".
If your seeded sessions produce a different figure, use that one.

## Concept C — "Nine behaviours"

**The insight:** pure utility. No emotional hook, just the thing people are searching for.
Lowest scroll-stopping power, highest intent from whoever does stop.

| Time | Shot | On screen text | VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Text on brand background | "The 9 Civil Service Behaviours" | "There are nine Civil Service Behaviours." |
| 2.0–6.0 | Animated list, three at a time | Behaviour names | "Most candidates can name three. You're marked on all nine." |
| 6.0–10.0 | **CAPTURE** — practice on a behaviour question | — | "Practise the ones you'd struggle to evidence." |
| 10.0–13.0 | **CAPTURE** — feedback panel | — | "And find out before an assessor does." |
| 13.0–15.0 | Logo + URL | "aicareermentor.co.uk" | "Free to start." |

**Cheapest to produce** — mostly text and screen capture, one or two AI shots at most.
Worth making because it costs least and speaks directly to the query set.

## Where these should land

**Not the homepage.** An ad about Civil Service scoring that lands on a general interview
homepage will convert badly, and the ad spend will be blamed for what is really a
mismatch.

Two options:

1. **Land on `/blog/civil-service-interview-tips`** — now 1,851 words with a cluster
   beneath it. Free, already live, and continuous with the ad's promise. Weakness: it is
   an article, so the path to signup is a click away.
2. **Build a dedicated landing page** — Civil Service framing, the practice CTA above the
   fold, the cluster linked below. I can build and deploy this whenever you want; it is
   ordinary site work and costs no Higgsfield credits.

I would do (1) for the first test, because it costs nothing and tells you whether the
creative works at all. If click-through is decent and signups are not, that isolates the
landing page as the problem and (2) becomes worth building.

**Tag everything.** The signup attribution tracking already in the admin analytics will
report channel, so use UTMs consistently: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=cs-autumn&utm_content=concept-a`.

## Production notes

**Credit cost.** A Marketing Studio ad at 15 seconds with audio preflighted at
**75 credits**. Three concepts in 9:16 only is ~225. Adding 16:9 cuts doubles it, so
derive those with `reframe` instead, which is far cheaper than regenerating.

**Reuse the demo footage.** Every CAPTURE shot above already exists in the candidate demo
shot list. Record once, use in both. This is the main reason to do the demos first.

**Do not fabricate numbers.** "You scored a 3" is a plausible scenario, not a claim about
a real person. But the filler count, the dimension scores and anything shown in the
interface must be genuine output from a real session, for the same reason the case study
now uses fictional companies.

**Test one before making three.** Run Concept A, look at retention and click-through,
then decide. Producing all three before seeing a single result spends credits on
assumptions.

---

## Honest note on sequencing

The site currently gets four clicks a week. Paid traffic into that will mostly buy you a
clearer picture of a conversion rate you have not yet tested.

None of this is wasted — the scripts keep, and the cluster is compounding on its own. But
if the ads underperform, the likeliest cause is not the creative, it is that the landing
experience has never been tested at volume. Concept A landing on the existing article is
the cheapest way to find that out before committing to a full campaign.

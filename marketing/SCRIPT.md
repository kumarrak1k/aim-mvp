# Demo video scripts & storyboards

Two videos — one per audience. Both are **problem-first** (hook in the first ~8s),
mirror the real user journey, one idea per scene, and end on a single CTA.
Narration is written to sound **confident and calm, not salesy**. UK English.

**Production notes**
- Voice: warm, credible, unhurried (≈150 wpm). A neutral British voice suits the brand.
- Music: subtle, modern, low bed; lift slightly at the CTA.
- Captions: burn in the on-screen text (most people watch muted). Keep to ≤6 words.
- Footage: use the framed screenshots in `marketing/framed/` for static beats and the
  raw clips in `marketing/clips/` for motion beats. Hold each static shot 2.5–3.5s with a
  slow push-in (Ken Burns) so it doesn't feel like a slideshow.
- Brand: never use "Ltd" or imply incorporation. Keep claims to what's shown on screen.
- ⚠️ The **voice + camera delivery** scene needs a shot of a real person on camera with
  good delivery scores (the synthetic capture shows a test pattern). Record ~6s of a
  presentable person doing a camera answer, or use a licensed stock clip. Everything else
  is covered by the captured assets.

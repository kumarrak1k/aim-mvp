# Listicle outreach pack

Goal: get AI Career Mentor added to the roundup articles that already rank on
page 1 for "best AI interview practice tools" and similar queries. One
inclusion in a ranking listicle puts you on page 1 by proxy, and these
articles are exactly what ChatGPT and Perplexity cite when recommending
tools.

How to use: find the author (byline on the article, or the site's editor
email), personalise the three [bracketed] parts, send from
rakesh@aicareermentor.co.uk. Batch of 5 to 10 per week. Track replies in a
simple sheet.

Where to find targets: Google "best AI interview practice tools", "AI mock
interview tools", "interview preparation apps 2026". Prioritise articles on
career sites, student sites and HR blogs that appear on page 1 or 2.

## Personalisation checklist (do all three, 2 minutes per email)

1. Name the article and say one specific true thing about it.
2. Check the article's date: if it says "2025" suggest they may be
   refreshing it for 2026 (editors update ranking listicles regularly, and
   an update is the natural moment to add a tool).
3. If their list has a structure (price column, "best for" tags), offer
   the exact row data so adding you is zero work: price from £0, best for
   UK candidates and assessment centre prep, free tier yes.

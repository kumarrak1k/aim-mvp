# Main advert — your voice: recording brief

## The sample (record this first — just these two lines)

Before recording everything, we test tone and pace on two lines:

> **Line 1 (the hook):** "You know you're good. So why don't interviews show it?"
>
> **Line 4 (a product line):** "Instant scores on your answers, delivery and presence."

Read each one **three times in a single recording**, with a 2-second pause
between takes. Vary them slightly: first take conversational, second a touch
slower and warmer, third with a bit more energy. That gives us a range to
pick the register from before you record the rest.

Save it as `sample.m4a` (or .wav/.mp3) into:
`aim-mvp\marketing\social\vo-rakesh\raw\`

## How to record

- Phone voice-memo app is fine — often better than a laptop mic.
- Small room, soft furnishings (bedroom beats kitchen), no fan/traffic hum.
- Phone about 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off to the side (kills pops).
- Stand or sit upright; smile slightly on the product lines — it's audible.
- Don't announce the lines; just leave a beat of silence before each take.

## Pace guide

Speak naturally — the video re-times around your read, so don't rush to hit
these. They're a feel guide (the current read's timings):

| # | Line | Target feel |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | "You know you're good. So why don't interviews show it?" | ~3.5s — direct, like a question to a friend |
| 2 | "Knowing your stuff isn't enough. You've got to deliver it." | ~3s — the truth, not a lecture |
| 3 | "Practise real interviews, any time." | ~2s — light, easy |
| 4 | "Instant scores on your answers, delivery and presence." | ~2.8s — confident, even |
| 5 | "And a stronger answer to learn from." | ~2.2s — warm |
| 6 | "Practise. Improve. Go again." | ~2.5s — three beats, tiny pauses |
| 7 | "Then walk in ready." | ~1.5s — quiet confidence, the payoff |
| 8 | "AI Career Mentor. Start free." | ~2.4s — clean, unhurried sign-off |

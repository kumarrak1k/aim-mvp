# Recording session — everything in one sitting

Four sections, ~35 lines, 10–15 minutes of recording. Section A is the
website advert; B–D power the new feed-native formats. Record them all
now and we won't need another session for weeks — every future video
gets cut from this bank.

**Setup reminders:** Voice Memos on Lossless, enhancement off, aeroplane
mode, soft room, 15–20cm off-axis, stand up. **One second of silence
before and after every line.** 2–3 takes per line, vary the energy.
Record one file per section (`sectionA.m4a` … `sectionD.m4a`) and drop
them in `aim-mvp\marketing\social\vo-rakesh\raw\`. If you fluff a line,
don't stop — pause a beat and read the whole line again.

## B · Watch It Score #1 (the lead new format)

Narration over a screen recording of the app scoring a weak answer (4)
then a strong one (9). Question on screen: "Tell me about a time you
failed." Conversational — you're showing a mate something interesting,
not presenting.

| # | Line | Delivery |
|---|------|----------|
| B1 | "This is what a four out of ten interview answer looks like." | Hook variant 1 — matter-of-fact, almost amused |
| B2 | "I asked an AI to score my interview answer. It didn't hold back." | Hook variant 2 — record both, we test which wins |
| B3 | "The question: tell me about a time you failed." | Set-up, neutral |
| B4 | "Here's the answer most people give." | Slight knowing tone |
| B5 | "Sounds fine, right? It scored a four." | The turn — pause after "right?" |
| B6 | "No real story. No ownership. No result. That's the waffle trap." | Three jabs then the label |
| B7 | "Same question. Stronger answer." | Quick, moving on |
| B8 | "A specific story. Real numbers. What changed after." | Mirror of B6, but approving |
| B9 | "That's a nine." | Let it land — short |
| B10 | "The difference isn't talent. It's practice." | The thesis — this is the line of the video |
